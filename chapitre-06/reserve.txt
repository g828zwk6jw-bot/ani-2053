Why: Creating 200 separate threads has a cost because the operating system must create, schedule, and manage them. A thread pool reuses existing worker threads, which can reduce overhead for many short tasks. When each task becomes 100 times longer, the actual computation takes a larger share of the total time, so thread-creation overhead matters less.
